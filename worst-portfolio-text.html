About Me

Hi. My name is Mike. I am a designer. I design things. Sometimes those things are good. You're looking at one of the times they are not. I have mass quantities of creative vision and I once used a computer. I am proficient in Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe InDesign, Microsoft Word, and the Microwave. I am a team player who works well independently. I am also a hard worker who is hardly working. Thank you for visiting my website. Please do not contact my references.
My Projects
Brand Identity Work

I made logos for companies. Some of them even liked the logos. One time a client said "can you make it pop more" and I simply blacked out for 11 hours. When I came to, the logo had 47 gradients and a bevel effect. The client loved it. I have never recovered. I specialize in making sure the kerning is slightly off so you feel uneasy but can't explain why.

Music Video (Capstone Project)

For my capstone I directed a music video. It has cinematography. It has editing. It has a timeline in Premiere Pro that would make a grown man weep. Not from beauty. From the 87 unlabeled layers. I spent four months on this and my professor gave me an A which means either it was genuinely good or he was tired. The video contains visual storytelling, emotional resonance, and at least one shot that was slightly out of focus but I convinced everyone it was "intentional atmosphere." This is my magnum opus and I will never shut up about it.

mPod — iPod MP3 Player App

I designed an app called mPod. It's an MP3 player that looks like an iPod because sometimes innovation means looking backwards with tremendous confidence. I did the UI/UX for it which means I decided where the buttons go. Do you know how many arguments you can have about where a single play button goes? I do. The answer is nine. Nine arguments. The click wheel was particularly challenging to design because click wheels haven't been relevant since 2007 but I simply did not care. Nostalgia is a valid design principle and I will die on this hill.

Photography

I take photos. Some of them are in focus. I shoot on a real camera because phone photography is valid but I need people at parties to ask me about my gear. My work explores themes of light, shadow, and "I was already at the location so I figured why not." I once spent forty-five minutes waiting for golden hour and then a cloud showed up. I have a Lightroom preset I made myself and I guard it like a state secret even though it's basically just +10 contrast and a slightly warm white balance. My photography has been described as "oh nice" by my mom.
